**Q1.** A patient has an abscess on the left thoracic region just distal to the left scapula that has been surgically incised to drain the abscess. The patient has a tubular defect that travels 2.4 in (6.1 cm) transversely under the skin toward the right scapula. With the patient in left sidelying position, which of the following statements **MOST** accurately represents the correct documentation of the defect?

A. Tunneling at 12 o'clock
B. Undermining at 12 o'clock
C. Tunneling at 3 o'clock
D. Undermining at 3 o'clock

**Correct Answer:**
- **C.** A tunnel is a channel that extends from any part of the wound through subcutaneous tissue or muscle. Undermining and tunneling are documented by measuring depth and noting the location using the face of the clock as a guide. Using the clock method, the patient's head is oriented at 12 o'clock and the feet toward 6 o'clock. Tunneling at 3 o'clock is correct, because the channel extends toward 3 o'clock.

**Incorrect Answers:**
- **A.** Using the clock method, the patient's head is oriented at 12 o'clock and the feet toward 6 o'clock. Tunneling at 12 o'clock is incorrect, because, regardless of the patient's position, the tunnel extends laterally toward 3 o'clock.
- **B.** Undermining is tissue destruction that occurs under intact skin around the wound perimeter. The undermining options are incorrect, because the wound described in the item stem is a channel, not a separation around the wound's perimeter.
- **D.** Undermining is tissue destruction that occurs under intact skin around the wound perimeter. The undermining options are incorrect, because the wound described in the item stem is a channel, not a separation around the wound's perimeter.

The oculomotor nerve (CN III) is also important in mediating the pupillary light reflex.

**Incorrect Answers:**
- **B.** The facial nerve (CN VII) innervates the muscles of facial expression. The signs and symptoms described in the scenario are consistent with involvement of the oculomotor nerve (CN III).
- **C.** The trigeminal nerve (CN V) mediates the jaw-jerk reflex. The signs and symptoms described in the scenario are consistent with involvement of the oculomotor nerve (CN III).
- **D.** The trigeminal nerve (CN V) mediates pain sensation from the face. The signs and symptoms described in the scenario are consistent with involvement of the oculomotor nerve (CN III).

C. Plantar aspect of the fifth toe

**Correct Answer:**
- **D.** An intervertebral disc protrusion compresses the nerve root numbered one spinal level inferior to the herniated disc. (pp. 141, 1494)

**Incorrect Answers:**
- **A.** The medial knee is part of the L₃ dermatome (p. 141).
- **B.** The popliteal fossa is part of the S₂ dermatome (p. 141).
- **C.** The plantar foot is part of the S₁ or S₂ dermatome (p. 141).
